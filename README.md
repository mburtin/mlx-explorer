# mlxSuite Explorer

Browse [MonolixSuite](https://www.simulations-plus.com/software/monolix/) projects and read their results in Positron.

## Features

- **Project list** — Scan and list in a sidebar all your **Monolix** and **Simulx** runs in the repository.
- **Summary page** — Summarize the most important information in your run (such as parameter estimations and likelihood criteria).
- **Model viewer** — Preview your model.
- **Data viewer** — Use the powerful Positron DataExplorer to explore and analyze the data used in your run.

## Requirements

Positron 2026.9+. You must have Monolix installed, with a valid license, to take full advantage of the tool.
